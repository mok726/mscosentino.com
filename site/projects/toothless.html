Exprimidor de dentífrico de ChimueloToothless toothpaste squeezer

Diseño 3D3D design

Exprimidor de pasta dental con la forma de Chimuelo, de “Cómo entrenar a tu dragón”, hecho a pedido de mi hijo.A toothpaste squeezer shaped like Toothless from “How to Train Your Dragon”, made at my son’s request.

Mi hijo quería un exprimidor de dentífrico de Chimuelo. Tomé como base el cortador de galletas “Toothless-cookie-cutter” de matschi, lo rellené y le hice varias secciones caladas para darle un aspecto más interesante.My son asked for a Toothless toothpaste squeezer. I used matschi’s “Toothless-cookie-cutter” as the base, filled it in (no longer a cookie cutter) and made several sections into holes to give it a more interesting look.

Me inspiré en el “Stormtrooper Toothpaste Squeezer” de Netsquash.I took inspiration from the “Stormtrooper Toothpaste Squeezer” by Netsquash.
