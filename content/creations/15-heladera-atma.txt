  en: The glass shelves rest on profiles (one at the front and one at the back) that slot into the side guides. Those profiles are very expensive, hard to find, and break over time.
- es: Lo que diseñé es una especie de escuadra que se encastra en la misma muesca de la guía y sostiene el estante. Son dos piezas: una lateral y otra trasera. No recuerdo el modelo exacto de la heladera.
  en: What I designed is a kind of bracket that slots into the same notch of the guide and holds the shelf. There are two parts: a side one and a rear one. I do not remember the exact refrigerator model.
